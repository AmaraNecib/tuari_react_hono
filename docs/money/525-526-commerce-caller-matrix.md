# Commerce exact-money caller and field matrix

This document maps the ten physical monetary tables named by [#525](https://github.com/AmaraNecib/tuari_react_hono/issues/525) and [#526](https://github.com/AmaraNecib/tuari_react_hono/issues/526) against the implementation and caller tests inspected at base `23e22ecf75a0cde7a5998673b90969683157ef68`. It records code structure and existing assertions. It does not claim that tests were executed here or that exact commerce is active in production.

The [exact-money contract](../adr/0001-exact-money-contract.md) defines scales and rounding. The older [#523 caller matrix](523-caller-matrix.md) describes the numeric baseline and shared non-commerce callers. The active numeric tables still use their legacy columns. Exact DDL below exists only for isolated fresh-copy fixtures and composition.

## Field matrix

Money2 means signed-int64 minor units stored as `INTEGER`, DZD-only. Price4 means non-negative signed-int64 units at four decimal places. Quantity remains independent six-decimal numeric stock, never an integer-monetary value.

| Table and exact field | Exact fixture shape | Source operand or meaning | Current exact adapter and writers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `invoice.total_amount_minor` | `INTEGER NOT NULL`, nonnegative int64 | Sum source line products or agreed line totals; round the document sum once, ties away from zero | `SqliteExactInvoiceRepository`; `ExactInvoiceService.createInvoice`, draft update and receive |
| `invoice.paid_amount_minor` | `INTEGER NOT NULL`, nonnegative int64 | Sum of live exact payment allocations; zero before payment | Invoice repository; `ExactInvoiceService.createPayment`, allocate and void paths |

The issue scope names ten tables: invoice and invoice_line_item have four monetary columns together, payment and payment_allocation have two, and inventory has nine across six tables. That is fifteen exact monetary fields in total. In exact inventory storage, the legacy `inventory_entry.total_price_dzd` column is removed in favor of required `price_source`; the exact source operands derive the line value, rather than storing a separately rounded line-total column. The physical `payment` amount is `payment.total_amount_minor`; `payment_allocation.amount_minor` is the per-invoice allocated amount. Both are Money2, non-null, strictly positive int64 values. The invoice monetary arithmetic also uses positive payment total with allocation conservation; unallocated cash is represented as payment total minus live allocation sum, not a fabricated debt settlement.

The exact fixture DDL is in [`exact-invoice-schema.ts`](../../apps/api/src/modules/invoice/adapters/exact-invoice-schema.ts) and [`exact-inventory-schema.ts`](../../apps/api/src/modules/inventory/adapters/exact-inventory-schema.ts). Those adapters assert required SQLite declared types at construction and check reads/writes. Inventory fixture DDL derives non-money columns from the current initialized definitions in [`schema-definitions.ts`](../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/database/schema-definitions.ts), then renames/replaces monetary columns. This is not an in-place production migration.

## Producer linkage, transaction ownership and replay

A received invoice owns its producer identity and links its invoice to the debt account and entry. A payment allocation links one exact payment to one invoice and the corresponding debt entry, with an immutable amount and allocation order. Voiding an invoice reverses only its recorded allocations; voiding a payment reverses its live allocations and leaves the payment identity/history intact. No sum derived from the ledger replaces the source invoice total.

Inventory purchase groups link to their debt account/entry. A credit purchase creates a `PAYABLE` obligation for the full gross amount; an explicit initial payment is a linked payment entry that reduces the obligation to the unpaid remainder. An outbound `SALE` creates a `RECEIVABLE` obligation. A `RETURN` creates a `PAYABLE` obligation. `GIFT` creates no debt. The producer fields identify the purchase group or outbound transfer. Later outbound payments use `recordOutboundPayment` through the inventory contract/router and targeted translated form. Remaining outbound debt is a query view of the original ledger entry, not a second persisted monetary cache. Generic debt payment remains forbidden for both owned domains. The exact debt service owns debt direction, immutable producer attribution and debt aggregate receipts; inventory owns stock availability, domain authorization and its aggregate receipt. Exact commerce composition supplies a shared database and FIFO lock so a domain write, ledger mutation and durable receipt share one synchronous transaction. On failure the whole aggregate rolls back; the same canonical intent and actor replays its result, while changed intent/actor conflicts. Transaction IDs may vary per attempt without changing replay identity.

Asset purchase value is not a second project contribution. A placement charge is a separate contribution; parked asset value is excluded from contribution totals. Consumable contribution follows consumed/project-stock quantity and transfer history, not a second copy of the purchase. Outbound quantity consumes only selected available stock, and transfer valuation keeps its source operands. Internal movement creates no new money or debt. Read-only material reports use `sumExactLineHistory`, with one final rounding and no document-count or int64 result limit. Already-finalized report totals combine through `sumMoneyReports`; bounded `Money` and `finalizeLines` remain the persistence/document interfaces. Native list adapters reject oversized collections before hydration or filtering rather than returning a prefix. Invoice/payment date cursors compare actual offset-aware instants and fractional seconds, not raw timestamp text.

## #336 overlap, not blanket closure

Issue [#336](https://github.com/AmaraNecib/tuari_react_hono/issues/336) calls out field-bound cursor type validation, duplicate payment IDs returning a typed failure, fractional allocation sums, and `deleteIfUnallocated` joining an outer transaction. The exact invoice service test explicitly asserts money-sort cursor reuse against a different sort falls back to the full list, exact decimal allocations `0.10 + 0.20` reconcile to `0.30`, and ordered remainder distribution conserves `100.00`. This does not prove generic legacy `payment.module` duplicate-ID behavior or `deleteIfUnallocated` transaction joining. Do not claim blanket #336 closure; track those paths against their own acceptance and tests.
