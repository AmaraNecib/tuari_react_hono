## Defender defenses (all upheld)

- **D1 (9/10)** — File move matches the ports & adapters convention: every storage
  adapter in the app lives under `<module>/adapters/` (workforce, company,
  invoice, payment, auth, update, inventory); rbac's `sqlite-role.repository.ts`
  was the last root-level sqlite adapter.
- **D2 (9/10)** — `role.module.ts` stays at root: it is the deep module owning the
  seam; deep modules live at root everywhere (`employee.module.ts`, …). The
  issue's operative directive scopes to *storage adapters*.
- **D3 (9/10)** — Constructor narrowing to `AppDrizzle` is "accept dependencies,
  don't create them": removes the raw-handle union, the force-cast, and the
  `createDrizzleFromSqlite` coupling (#191's target); matches `DrizzleAccountRepository(db)`
  etc.
- **D4 (8/10)** — Behavior identity on the default path: composition root builds
  one `db` from the shared handle; better-sqlite3 is a single synchronous
  connection, so a second Drizzle wrapper was never a second connection.
- **D5 (8/10)** — Adding the unit test for the previously-untested SQL adapter is
  justified (move is the natural moment to pin behavior).

## Dismissed / out of scope (documented, not fixed)

- `company/sqlite-company.repository.ts` at the company module root is a legacy
  artifact predating the adapters/ pattern (company/adapters/ holds the newer
  sqlite-* ports) — out of scope for #209 (defender's note; pre-existing).
- C5 style nit — dismissed (see table).
