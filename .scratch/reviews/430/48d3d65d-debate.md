## Disproved by the critic (kept as safeguards)

- Writes are not blocked during the copy and WAL cannot corrupt the snapshot:
  `journal_mode = WAL`, the copy runs on a dedicated connection, and the
  writer-held test passes.
- Retention never prunes the newest or a foreign folder: explicit keep-newest
  guard plus `isUsableSnapshot` re-check before every `rm`.
- No SQL injection via the snapshot path: `escapeSqliteString` doubles quotes
  and the path is generated by `mkdtempSync`.

## Defender's weakest points (accepted)

- Encryption is unexercised (#410) — labelled, not claimed.
- `lastFailure` is per-process; the durable signal is the newest folder date —
  documented.
- Readiness is coupled to `/health` plus a 30s fallback — matches the shell's
  probe.

No finding was dismissed on "probably fine" without a code path or test.
