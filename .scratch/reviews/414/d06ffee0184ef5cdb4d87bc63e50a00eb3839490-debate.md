## Defender summary (confidence)

- D1 dedicated read-model port is the right seam (7/10): four sources in three
  modules, names the owning modules do not expose project-scoped; two real
  adapters (sqlite + test fakes); the interface is the test surface.
- D2 parked inventory out of the total is correct and structurally impossible
  to leak (9/10): parked total is computed after `totalDzd` is fixed.
- D3 the four sources are disjoint by construction (8/10): different table +
  different key per part; negative cases pinned.
- D4 `roundMoney` + the bound guard prevent drift and unrepresentable output
  (9/10): round-once salary math is the right trade.
- D5 the UI communicates the number honestly (7/10): four labelled rows with
  money + share + bar, parked in its own labelled surface, the three empty-state
  actions present.

## Verdict

The design stands. The one ship-affecting business question (#1) is the
documented ASK; #2, #6, #16 and #17 were fixed in `d06ffee0`.
