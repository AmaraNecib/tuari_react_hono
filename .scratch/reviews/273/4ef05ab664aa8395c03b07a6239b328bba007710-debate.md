## Defender position

- **Resilience:** the app never hard-fails when the OS store is missing (headless
  Linux, locked keychain) — it degrades to an owner-only plaintext fallback,
  preserving the pre-#273 behavior while adding OS protection where available.
- **Migration:** pre-#273 installs keep their existing `better-auth.secret` /
  `app-db.key` — the plaintext is adopted into the OS store and deleted, so no
  re-key or re-auth is forced on upgrade.
- **No new dependencies:** `keyring` was already a Cargo dependency (used for the
  desktop bearer token); `node:crypto` is built-in.
- **Defense in depth:** BETTER_AUTH_SECRET is enforced at BOTH the exported Zod
  schema and the factory; the setup key at the factory (NODE_ENV-conditional);
  the comparison is hash-then-`timingSafeEqual` (no length or early-exit oracle);
  the RPC rate limiter remains as the throttling layer.
- **E2E:** the setup-key gate is exercised over the wire with the exact Arabic
  message, and the entire suite stays green with the key enabled globally.

No blockers. Two accepted residuals (C1 transient flip window, C4 Windows
