## Defender position

- **Resilience:** the app never hard-fails when the OS store is missing (headless
  Linux, locked keychain) — it degrades to an owner-only plaintext fallback,
  preserving the pre-#273 behavior while adding OS protection where available.
- **Migration:** pre-#273 installs keep their existing `better-auth.secret` /
  `app-db.key` — the plaintext is adopted into the OS store and deleted, so no
  re-key or re-auth is forced on upgrade.
- **No new dependencies:** `keyring` was already a Cargo dependency (used for the
  desktop bearer token); `node:crypto` is built-in.
- **Defense in depth:** BETTER_AUTH_SECRET is enforced at BOTH the exported Zod
  schema and the factory; the setup key at the factory (NODE_ENV-conditional);
  the comparison is hash-then-`timingSafeEqual` (no length or early-exit oracle);
  the RPC rate limiter remains as the throttling layer.
- **E2E:** the setup-key gate is exercised over the wire with the exact Arabic
  message, and the entire suite stays green with the key enabled globally.

## Post-evidence delta (round 2, `1dadd31`) — entropy gate calibration

Greptile (first review of PR #306) flagged a **real blocker**: the original
`>= 4.0 bits/char` entropy gate sits at the THEORETICAL maximum for hex data,
but the empirical Shannon entropy of a FINITE random hex sample is
systematically below it (~3.6–3.9 bpc depending on length). The app's own
documented generation paths — the desktop's `OsRng` hex secrets (96/64 chars)
and `openssl rand -hex 32` (64 chars) — would therefore intermittently be
REJECTED at API startup (measured: 18.6% of 32-char hex, 0.9% of 48-char hex,
0.02% of 64-char hex fell below 4.0 bpc).

**Fix (commit `1dadd31`):**
- `MIN_SECRET_ENTROPY_BITS_PER_CHAR` lowered 4.0 → **2.0**.
- Added `MIN_SECRET_DISTINCT_CHARS = 8` diversity floor.
- Calibration verified by simulation: **P(bpc < 2.0) = 0 over 500k random hex
  samples at every documented length 20–96**; P(<2.0) < 2e-6 even at the
  20-char setup-key floor.
- Degenerate secrets are still rejected (single symbol → 0 distinct; `aabbccdd`
  blocks → 4 distinct < 8; both fail the diversity floor). The shipped
  placeholders pass the 2.0 floor but are caught by the exact-match blocklist
  (comment updated to state this — they are no longer caught by the gate).
- Regression test added: a realistic 66-char hex sample at 3.975 bpc (rejected
  by the old gate) is now accepted.

Debate verdict after delta: blocker resolved; the two original residuals (C1
transient keyring flip, C4 Windows fallback ACL) are unchanged and accepted.
