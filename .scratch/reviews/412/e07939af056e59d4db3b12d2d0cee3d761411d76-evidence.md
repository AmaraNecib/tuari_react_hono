## Spec points that were ambiguous

1. **Month length vs `/30`.** The helper counts real calendar days of the
   intersection, so a full-March assignment suggests 31 days while `days`
   defaults to 30. The form surfaces the `-1` remainder (over-allocation) rather
   than hiding it. If the parent wants a full period to always suggest 30, the
   derivation needs a normalization rule (e.g. coverage ratio × 30).
2. **`expectedAmountDzd` meaning.** It now stores the prorated proposal rather
   than the raw wage; `actualAmountDzd` remains the authoritative paid amount.
3. **Days are whole numbers** — enforced at the wire and in the service. The
   issue does not say whether halves are legal; the "business types whole days"
   reading was applied.
4. **Details-page quick payment** (outside the salaries section) sends
   `days: 1` for DAILY and `30` for others to preserve its stored expectations;
   it has no split editor.
