## Decision

Use a compact account statement followed by one operation, not nine equal statistics. Show wages owed to employee and advances recoverable by company as independent facts. Keep opening provenance separate and never net these directions. Preserve server-provided summary arithmetic. Put the other accounting figures in a named disclosure. Keep history accessible below the operation. Remove introductory educational copy.

Reviewed opening balances are historical provenance. Show reviewed amounts with a correction disclosure, not a permanently expanded competing editor. Unreviewed data, pending requests, refund proposals and uncertain responses remain visible outside that disclosure. Keep the existing preview and explicit cash confirmation intact.

Payment controls retain existing payload construction and persisted retry coordination. The repay eligibility check must accept a selected reviewed opening advance. Offset still requires both a current earning and current advance; opening sources cannot be offset. Disabled source-dependent actions explain the missing source next to the button.
